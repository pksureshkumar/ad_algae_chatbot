================================================================================
EVALUATION QUESTIONS — AD/Algae Chatbot Benchmark
================================================================================
Total: 90 questions across 3 tiers
Tier 1:  20 questions — Factual retrieval (specific numbers from specific papers)
Tier 2:  60 questions — Cross-study synthesis (existing batch queries)
Tier 3:  10 questions — Cross-paper reasoning (requires comparing across studies)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TIER 1 — FACTUAL RETRIEVAL (20 questions)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Tests whether the system can retrieve a specific numerical value from
a specific paper. GPT-direct will hallucinate or give vague ranges. Text-only
RAG may miss values buried in tables. Multimodal RAG should retrieve exact
figures including those extracted from tables by MinerU.

Scoring focus: numerical specificity, citation accuracy, faithfulness.

T1-01. What methane yield (in mL CH4/g VS) was reported for Chlorella vulgaris
       under mesophilic anaerobic digestion conditions, and what pretreatment
       method was used?

T1-02. What digestate dilution ratio was used to cultivate microalgae on
       anaerobic digestion effluent in studies using high rate algal ponds
       (HRAP), and what was the resulting biomass productivity?

T1-03. What methane content (% v/v) was achieved during biogas upgrading using
       an algal-bacterial photobioreactor at pilot scale, and over what time
       period was this maintained?

T1-04. What volatile fatty acid (VFA) concentration (g/L) was reported for
       anaerobic co-digestion of microalgae with food waste, and what was the
       acetate fraction of total VFAs?

T1-05. What was the reported cost ($/kg or €/kg) for microalgae biomass
       production in a techno-economic analysis of an integrated photobioreactor
       and anaerobic digestion system?

T1-06. What free ammonia (NH3) concentration was identified as inhibitory to
       Chlorella vulgaris growth, and at what pH was this measured?

T1-07. What improvement in methane yield (%) was reported when algal biochar
       was added to anaerobic co-digestion of algal biomass and food waste
       under mesophilic conditions?

T1-08. What organic loading rate (g VS/L/d) and hydraulic retention time (days)
       were used in long-term anaerobic digestion of microalgae grown in a high
       rate algal pond (HRAP)?

T1-09. What biogas methane content was achieved by simultaneous biogas upgrading
       and centrate treatment in an outdoor pilot-scale HRAP, and what CO2
       removal efficiency was reported?

T1-10. What net energy ratio or energy return on investment was reported for an
       integrated microalgae-anaerobic digestion system, and what system
       boundary was used in the calculation?

T1-11. What was the reported hydrogen yield (mL H2/g VS or similar) from a
       bio-electrochemical system (BES) or microbial electrolysis cell (MEC)
       combined with anaerobic digestion?

T1-12. What lipid content (% dry weight) was reported for Chlorella vulgaris
       grown using CO2 from biogas as the carbon source compared to air-fed
       controls?

T1-13. What specific methane yield was reported for anaerobic digestion of
       Tetraselmis sp. or another marine microalgae, and how did it compare
       to the theoretical biochemical methane potential?

T1-14. What removal efficiency (%) for nitrogen and phosphorus was achieved
       when microalgae were cultivated on anaerobic digestion effluent in a
       photobioreactor or HRAP system?

T1-15. What acetate production rate or titer was reported in a study using
       microbial electrosynthesis or bio-electrochemical systems for CO2
       reduction combined with anaerobic processes?

T1-16. What was the reported seasonal variation in methane content of upgraded
       biogas in an outdoor algal-bacterial photobioreactor system, and what
       was the range across seasons?

T1-17. What inoculum-to-substrate ratio (I:S, g VS/g VS) was used in
       biochemical methane potential (BMP) tests for microalgae feedstocks,
       and which BMP protocol was followed?

T1-18. What polyhydroxyalkanoate (PHA) or polyhydroxybutyrate (PHB) yield
       (% cell dry weight) was reported for microalgae or cyanobacteria under
       nutrient stress conditions?

T1-19. What was the reported energy consumption (kWh/m3 biogas or similar) for
       membrane-based CO2 capture or upgrading of biogas to biomethane?

T1-20. What organic loading rate and methane yield were reported for an upflow
       anaerobic sludge blanket (UASB) reactor treating microalgae-based
       wastewater or microalgal biomass?

TIER 3 — CROSS-PAPER REASONING (10 questions)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Requires synthesizing and comparing data across multiple papers.
Correct answers almost always depend on data in tables or figures, not just
abstract text. This is where the largest gap between multimodal and text-only
is expected. Answers should cite multiple papers and compare specific values.

Scoring focus: all four metrics, but especially faithfulness and hallucination.

T3-01. Across studies that applied pretreatment before anaerobic digestion of
       microalgae, which pretreatment method (thermal, ultrasound, enzymatic,
       chemical, or mechanical) shows the best net energy balance when
       pretreatment energy cost is accounted for, and what methane yield
       improvements were reported?

T3-02. Comparing studies on algae-AD integration, what digestate dilution
       factor is most commonly used before cultivating microalgae on AD
       effluent, and how does this relate to free ammonia inhibition thresholds
       reported for different species?

T3-03. Across techno-economic assessments of integrated microalgae-anaerobic
       digestion systems, what is the range of reported production costs or
       levelized costs of energy, and what assumptions most commonly explain
       the variation between studies?

T3-04. Comparing HRAP-based biogas upgrading studies, what methane content
       (% v/v) is consistently achievable in upgraded biogas, and how does
       performance vary between indoor lab-scale and outdoor pilot-scale
       systems?

T3-05. Across studies using bio-electrochemical systems (BES) or microbial
       electrolysis cells (MEC) combined with anaerobic digestion, what
       applied voltage or energy input was used and what improvement in
       methane or hydrogen yield was reported relative to conventional AD?

T3-06. Looking at studies that co-digest microalgae with other substrates
       (food waste, manure, sewage sludge), which co-substrate combination
       consistently produces the highest methane yield improvement, and what
       inoculum-to-substrate ratio was used?

T3-07. Across lifecycle assessment (LCA) studies on microalgae-AD integrated
       systems, what global warming potential (GWP, kg CO2-eq per unit energy
       or product) is reported, and which system configurations achieve net
       negative GWP?

T3-08. Comparing studies on algal biochar addition to anaerobic digestion,
       what biochar dosage (g/L or % VS) produced the greatest methane yield
       improvement, and was this consistent across mesophilic and thermophilic
       conditions?

T3-09. Across studies that report VFA composition (not just total VFA) from
       anaerobic fermentation or co-digestion involving algae, what is the
       typical acetate-to-propionate ratio and how does algae addition affect
       the VFA profile compared to controls without algae?

T3-10. Looking at studies that simultaneously treat wastewater and produce
       biogas or biomethane using integrated microalgae-AD systems, what
       nutrient removal efficiencies (N and P) are reported alongside energy
       outputs, and which system configuration achieves the best balance of
       both?

================================================================================
SCORING RUBRIC (apply to every answer from all 3 systems)
================================================================================

(a) Citation accuracy       0 = no citations or hallucinated DOIs/titles
                            1 = real papers cited but finding misattributed
                            2 = correct paper with correct specific finding

(b) Numerical specificity   0 = no numbers given
                            1 = approximate range only (e.g., "200-400 mL/g VS")
                            2 = specific value with units and conditions reported

(c) Faithfulness            Verify 3 specific claims per answer against source
                            0 = <50% of claims verifiable in cited papers
                            1 = 50-80% verifiable
                            2 = >80% verifiable

(d) Hallucination flag      1 = at least one specific claim cannot be traced
                                to any real paper (binary, any answer)
                            0 = no hallucinations detected

Scorer: blind to which system produced each answer.
Max score per answer: 6 (for a-c). Track (d) separately as a rate.
